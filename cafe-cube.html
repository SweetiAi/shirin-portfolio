01.

The Problem

Traditional café menus are static and overwhelming. Users often spend too much time deciding what to order and rarely get recommendations that truly match their mood or needs.

Decision Fatigue

Too many options make it hard to choose.

Lack of Personalization

Menus don't adapt to your mood or preferences.

Transactional Experience

Ordering feels like a task, not an experience.

02.

Design Goal

To create a café experience that feels like chatting with a personal barista who understands you, recommends the right items, and makes ordering effortless and enjoyable.

Design Principles

Creating a conversational café experience that feels intelligent, friendly and effortless.

04.

Key Features

  • AI Concierge (Cube Chef): Chat with AI to get deeply personalized dietary recommendations.
  • Mood & Preference Based Suggestions: Tailored perfectly to how you feel, your allergens, and your immediate physical environment.
  • Smart Menu Browsing: An adaptive, highly graphical menu prioritizing emotional satisfaction over rows of lists.
  • Seamless Ordering: Quick add, rapid nutritional customization, and an integrated, single-tap checkout.
